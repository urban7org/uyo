Who we are

Forty years in one neighborhood

Urban Youth Outreach has been at work in Englewood, on the South Side of Chicago, since 1986. We do not parachute in. We have feet on the ground, we know our neighbors by name, and we stay. The work is slow and relational: one person at a time comes to want change, and then (God being gracious) it spreads to the rest of the house.

Today that work takes two forms. Urban Youth Outreach is the parent organization of Archer Christian Academy, a classical Christian school that opens its doors in the fall of 2027. The neighborhood outreach we founded, Englewood Family Outreach, carries on under Englewood Works.

Our school

Archer Christian Academy

A Christ-centered, classical Christian school in Englewood, governed by the Urban Youth Outreach Board of Directors. Archer opens in the fall of 2027 with kindergarten through second grade, adds a grade each year through twelfth, and keeps every class to fourteen students.

Founded by Urban Youth Outreach

Englewood Family Outreach

For years our neighbors knew us by this name. The outreach we founded among the children and families of Englewood continues today under the care of Englewood Works.

englewoodfamily.org →

Independent nonprofit

Englewood Works

A separate 501(c)(3) organization that equips Englewood residents with employability skills, mentorship, and hands-on training. Englewood Works is now the home of Englewood Family Outreach and its after-school club.
